## Design Specificity Verdict

Awesome Copilot Indonesia is authentically grounded in the developer experience with interactive console mockups, SDLC workflow steppers, dual-language switching (EN/ID), and copyable prompt templates. However, there is noticeable design system fragmentation between documented intent (DESIGN.md Airbnb tokens) and stylesheet implementation (main.css Tailwind Slate + Catppuccin terminal colors).

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Design System & Color Token Fragmentation: main.css diverged from DESIGN.md by introducing Indigo (#6366f1) and Slate (#0f172a), with stray #dd7878 in navbar underline.
- [P1] Choice Overload & Working Memory Violation: 21 TDD cards and 12 agent tabs are presented simultaneously without search or progressive disclosure.
- [P2] Accessibility (a11y) & Focus Stripping: Focus ring suppressed on accordion, copy buttons lack aria-live feedback and descriptive labels, color contrast on #6366f1 is 4.47:1.
- [P2] i18n Key Duplication & Binding Collision: Duplicate keys in translations.js and binding collision on SDLC Bypass Option B (prompt_gmd_3).
- [P3] Mobile Touch Targets: Mobile copy buttons are ~22-24px, navbar dropdowns are 38px (< 44px standard).

## Persona Red Flags

- Alex (Power User): No keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+K) to jump directly to slash commands; must click 12 separate buttons to explore agent templates.
- Jordan (First-Timer): Overwhelmed by 33+ slash commands and advanced engineering jargon without a 3-step quickstart wizard.
- Sam (Accessibility): Copy buttons lack aria-live announcements; focus indicators explicitly suppressed on mobile accordion buttons.
