# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Indonesian developers, international developers, and software engineering teams using AI assistants (like Copilot, Cursor, etc.). Their context is daily coding, planning, and code review tasks, and they are looking to standardize and accelerate their Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

## Product Purpose
A curated collection of AI agents and SDLC workflows to enhance development productivity and consistency. Success is seamless integration into developer workflows and achieving a standardized SDLC framework for AI-assisted development.

## Positioning
The most comprehensive collection of AI agents and SDLC workflows specifically tailored for Indonesian developers, combined with a multi-platform AI agent configuration toolkit with deep SDLC integration.

## Brand Personality
Professional, efficient, and comprehensive. The design aesthetic and documentation structure is inspired by Airbnb.

## Anti-references
Avoid overly playful or "cartoonish" designs. Avoid cluttered enterprise dashboards.

## Design Principles
- **Practice what you preach:** Tools for developers should be well-structured, consistent, and clearly documented.
- **Expert Confidence:** The presentation should instill trust, reliability, and technical competence.
- **Show, don't tell:** Provide clear examples, templates, and configurations that are ready for immediate use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance, focus on readability and keyboard navigation.
